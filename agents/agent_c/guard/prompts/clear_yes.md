The caller answered the robot's yes-or-no question with a clear yes to it, without doubt. It can be only a yes, or a yes together with a separate question or request about something else ("haan, aur rider ka number bhi chahiye"). It is not a clear yes when the yes depends on a condition or a doubt about this change ("haan, lekin SMS aayega na?"), when the caller asks something about this change before agreeing, when the caller says no, stop or wait, corrects what the robot said, asks to hear it again, or says words that don't answer the question.
